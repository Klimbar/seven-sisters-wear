Where Tradition Meets Timeless Artistry

In the heart of Assam, where the Brahmaputra flows gently past ancient weaving villages, our family's tradition of silk weaving has been passed down through five generations.

Each Mekhela Chador tells a story—not just of intricate patterns and natural dyes, but of dedication, patience, and an unwavering commitment to preserving the dying art of traditional Assamese weaving.
