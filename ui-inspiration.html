The Artisans

Master Craftspeople of North East

Our weavers and artisans come from every corner of North East India — from the Bodo weavers of Assam to the Naga tribes of Nagaland, from Manipuri weavers to Mizo artisans.

Each brings decades of expertise, using traditional handloom techniques passed down through generations, creating fabrics of unparalleled beauty and cultural significance.

  • 100% Handwoven Using Traditional Looms
  • Natural Dyes Derived from Indigenous Plants
  • Each Piece Takes 4-8 Weeks to Complete
  • Authentic Tribal Certifications
